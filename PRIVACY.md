# Peekaboo Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 20 November 2026_

Peekaboo is a macOS app by ORCA MEDIA GROUP that adds Quick Look previews and Finder thumbnails
for additional file types.

## What Peekaboo collects

Nothing. Peekaboo has no accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting, and it does not
send your files or any information about them to us or to anyone else.

## How your files are handled

- Files are read only when you preview them or when Finder asks for a thumbnail, and only on your
  Mac. Peekaboo never changes your files.
- To play MIDI and tracker music, Peekaboo converts the song to audio and keeps that audio in its
  own private cache on your Mac so the next preview starts faster. macOS may clear this cache at
  any time; deleting the app removes it.
- Your settings (which file types are on, the theme) are stored on your Mac only.

## Network use

Peekaboo makes no network requests of its own. The one exception is Markdown documents that
include images hosted on the web: like any Markdown viewer, the preview loads those images from
the addresses written in the document, so those servers see a normal image request from your Mac.
Peekaboo blocks scripts in previews from making any other requests.

## Updates

The Mac App Store version is updated by the App Store. The version downloaded from our website
checks a file on GitHub once a day for new versions; that check sends only what any web request
sends (your IP address and the app's version) and no personal data.

## Children

Peekaboo collects no data from anyone, including children.
